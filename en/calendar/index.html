How to add it

Two minutes, once.

  1. Fill your listAdd your shows, the movies you want to see and the games you're waiting for in Playwatch.
  2. Create your calendarProfile → Settings → Calendar → “Create my calendar”.
  3. Add it to your calendar app“Add to Google Calendar” in one tap, or “Copy link” for Outlook and other calendar apps.
  4. That's itEvery new show you follow and every newly announced date lands in your calendar automatically.

What's in your calendar

Only what you care about: releases from your list, nothing else.

Episodes of your showsEvery episode with its season, number and network, from last month up to 4 months ahead. The ones you've seen are marked “✓ Already watched”.
Movies on your listTheatrical and streaming releases, using your country's dates.
Games you're waiting forRelease dates for the games in your backlog.
Spoiler-freeWith spoiler-free mode on, titles and synopses of episodes you haven't seen stay hidden in your calendar too.
Always up to dateA date moves, an episode is added, you start a new show: your calendar follows without you doing anything.
A private linkYour calendar has a secret address. You can create a new one anytime and the old one stops working right away.

FAQ

Which calendar apps work with the Playwatch calendar?

Google Calendar (so the calendar on most Android phones, Samsung included), Outlook, and any calendar app that supports subscribing to an iCal (.ics) link.

How long until a new release shows up?

Playwatch refreshes the calendar several times a day. Google Calendar then re-reads subscriptions on its own schedule, which can add a few hours of delay.

Is the calendar free?

It's part of Playwatch Premium, a one-time €1.99 with no subscription. Premium also adds new-episode notifications, TV Time history import, advanced stats and themes.

Can anyone else see my calendar?

Only if you give them the link. It isn't published anywhere, and you can replace it with a new link from Playwatch's settings.
